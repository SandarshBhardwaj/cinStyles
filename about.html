About CinStyles

Style Is Personal. So Is My Story.

My Background

Eight Years. Hundreds of Wardrobes. One Philosophy.

My journey into styling didn't begin in a fashion school. It began in my mother's wardrobe. She had this extraordinary ability to put on a simple outfit and make the whole room shift. I spent years trying to understand what she was doing. Eventually, I realised it was intentionality.

After training across fashion capitals and building a clientele from corporate executives to creatives, I founded CinStyles with one goal: to help people stop dressing by default and start dressing with purpose. Every client I work with carries a distinct style identity, and my job is simply to excavate it.

I work with clients across Dubai, London, and beyond (in person and virtually) because good style shouldn't be geography-dependent. Whether you're redefining your professional image or stepping into a new chapter of life, I'm here for the whole journey.
